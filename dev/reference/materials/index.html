Material defaults

A Material holds the two surface properties the radiation model uses: the x-ray albedo (re-emitted over incident flux, a number or an albedo model) and the laser → x-ray conversion of the absorbed laser power (a number or a Conversion). The constructors gold, uranium, nif_gold, nif_uranium, ablator and BLACK build the package's own defaults.

material_library lists named material defaults whose every quantity has a published source or a derivation recorded in the research notes ("Material defaults and their sources (2026-11-18)"). Each entry, a MaterialDefault, carries the Material it builds and, per quantity, a SourcedValue: the value, how it was obtained (one of MATERIAL_BASES), its source and its uncertainty. Displaying an entry prints that record:

d = material_default(:gold)
display(d)                       # albedo and conversion, each with basis, source, uncertainty
t = hohlraum(; wall = d.material)

The entries named after a constructor build exactly the material that constructor builds, field for field, so a model built from the library gives the same results. The foil entries are the materials of foil_material, the default of foil and backlighter_foil: gold from published disk and sphere data, the mid-Z backlighter elements with the basis :suggested (a suggested range, not a measurement). Package defaults with no source found (the capsule's conversion 0.3 in ablator) are not in the library.

Briareus.FOIL_ELEMENTS — Constant
FOIL_ELEMENTS

The foil elements of foil_material, as (symbol, name, Z): gold first, then the backlighter elements of kshell_ce that are solid as pure elements (Ga, Br and Kr, a metal that melts at 30 °C, a liquid and a gas, are left out).

Briareus.MATERIAL_BASES — Constant
MATERIAL_BASES

How a SourcedValue was obtained:

  • :published: a measured value, a published scaling, or the input of a published model, as printed;
  • :range: a round value inside a published range, not itself a published number;
  • :calibrated: fitted in Briareus to the published data the source names;
  • :derived: Briareus's own arithmetic on published inputs;
  • :definition: true by construction (a perfect absorber);
  • :suggested: no published measurement exists; the value is a stated point (the midpoint) of a suggested range bracketed by published values of related materials or conditions, which source names and uncertainty gives. Not a measurement.
source
Briareus.MaterialDefault — Type
MaterialDefault

A named entry of material_library: name, a one-line description, the material it builds (a Material), the SourcedValues albedo and conversion the material is built from, and other, recorded quantities the radiation model does not use (laser absorption, cold reflectance; quantity => SourcedValue pairs). Printed with show (display(entry)), it lists every quantity with its basis, source and uncertainty.

source
Briareus.SourcedValue — Type
SourcedValue(value, basis, source, uncertainty)

One quantity of a MaterialDefault with its provenance: value (a number, an AlbedoModel or a Conversion), basis (how the value was obtained, one of MATERIAL_BASES), source (the citation, and the research-notes section that records it) and uncertainty (the stated or estimated spread, or the published values around it). source must not be empty: a value without a source does not enter the library.

Briareus.foil_material — Function
foil_material(element::Symbol = :Au) -> Material

The material of a directly irradiated foil (a backlighter) of element, one of FOIL_ELEMENTS: the material of the material_library entry :<name>_foil (:gold_foil, :titanium_foil, …), built without the library. The default of foil and backlighter_foil is foil_material().

elementalbedoconversionbasis
:Au0.80.8gold disks and spheres, static equivalent (below)
:Ti … :Ge, :Zr … :Ag0.55 (range 0.3–0.8)0.35 (range 0.1–0.6)suggested range, not a measurement
  • Gold: the conversion is Dewald et al.'s backscatter-corrected plateau at 10¹⁵ W/cm² (PoP 15, 072706 (2008); 0.95 at 10¹⁴), where backlighter foils are driven (He-α optima 1–5 × 10¹⁵ W/cm²); open 3ω gold disks gave 0.51–0.59 of the absorbed energy at 2 × 10¹³–4 × 10¹⁴ W/cm² (Mead et al., LA-UR-86-300 (1985)). The albedo is Lindl's wall loss at 1 ns, 0.76–0.85 over 150–300 eV. material_default(:gold_disk) keeps the intensity table, for solves that pass the laser power.
  • Mid-Z: no published 3ω broadband conversion was found for flat foils of these elements. The conversion range lies below gold's open-disk 0.51–0.59 and the 0.35–0.60 total conversion of laser-heated Fe-oxide aerogels at 351 nm (Pérez et al., PoP 19, 083101 (2012); steel-lined cavities 0.60–0.75), and its floor 0.1 under the 0.13–0.26 that Eidmann & Kishimoto's 2 % (Be) to ~50 % (heaviest) at 0.53 µm (APL 49, 377 (1986)) give linearly in Z over Z 22–47 (the measured dependence oscillates with Z). The albedo range lies between the static albedos of a low-Z ablator (0.3) and gold (0.8) (Young 2020). The value is each range's midpoint: a foil's x-ray emission is a wanted source in some designs and an unwanted background in others, so neither end is conservative.

The conversion here is the broadband laser → x-ray conversion (the radiosity source); the He-α line signal is kshell_ce's, fitted to its own data.

Briareus.material_default — Method
material_default(name::Symbol) -> MaterialDefault

The entry name of material_library; its material field is the Material. Throws an ArgumentError listing the names for an unknown one.

source
Briareus.material_library — Method
material_library() -> Vector{MaterialDefault}

The named material defaults whose every quantity has a published source, a derivation recorded in the research notes ("Material defaults and their sources (2026-11-18)") or, flagged with the basis :suggested, a suggested range bracketed by published values, freshly built on each call. Each entry is a MaterialDefault; material_default(name) returns one, and its material is the Material to use. The entries named after a constructor build exactly the material that constructor builds:

  • :gold: gold(), albedo 0.8 and conversion 0.9 (static solves): the gold wall of a published static VisRad model of a NIF hohlraum (Young et al., PoP 27, 082702 (2020));
  • :uranium: uranium(), the Au-lined DU wall, conversion 0.9;
  • :nif_gold, :nif_uranium: nif_gold(), nif_uranium(), the drive-history walls (Lindl's wall loss, the rising nif_conversion());
  • :gold_foil: foil_material(), the default of foil and backlighter_foil: directly irradiated gold, the static equivalent of :gold_disk at backlighter intensities (albedo 0.8, conversion 0.8);
  • :titanium_foil, :iron_foil, … (one per mid-Z element of FOIL_ELEMENTS): foil_material(element), both quantities :suggested (a suggested range, not a measurement);
  • :black: BLACK, the perfect absorber.

The others are published alternatives:

  • :gold_swadling2025: the only published VisRad energetics calibration (albedo 0.77, conversion 0.95 on a 5.75 mm gold hohlraum at 400 TW; Swadling et al., PoP 32, 052707 (2025));
  • :gold_disk: directly irradiated gold in open geometry (a foil or disk): Lindl's wall loss and Dewald et al.'s (PoP 15, 072706 (2008)) conversion plateau, 0.95 at 10¹⁴ and 0.80 at 10¹⁵ W/cm² (an intensity table), with the 3ω laser absorption and the 1ω cold reflectance recorded.

Package defaults without a source are not in the library: the capsule's laser → x-ray conversion 0.3 (ablator) and Material's own 0/0. ablator's albedo 0.3 has a source (Young 2020's static model) but its conversion has none, so the material is left out whole.
