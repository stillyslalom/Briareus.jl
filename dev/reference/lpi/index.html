Briareus.CalibratedTransfer — Type
CalibratedTransfer(donors, receivers, fraction)

Empirical cross-beam transfer: a fraction of the total power of the donors groups moves to the receivers groups (shared in proportion to each receiving beam's power). fraction(Δλ, t) is a calibration — e.g. fit to shot data — where Δλ = ⟨dlambda⟩_receivers − ⟨dlambda⟩_donors (Å, power-weighted means), so a positive Δλ means the receivers are red-shifted relative to the donors, the direction that drives transfer toward them. Total power is conserved.

Build fraction with linear_transfer or tabulated_transfer, or pass any differentiable function.

Briareus.BubbleAbsorption — Type
BubbleAbsorption(; radius, turn_on = 0.0, delay = 0.25, speed = 0.36, fill = 0.3,
                 energy = 1.0, opacity = 0.111, density_exponent = 1.8,
                 exponent = 0.5, x_ref = 1.0, stagnation = 0.80 radius,
                 stagnation_width = 0.08 radius, collision = <t_c>, capsule_radius = 0.908,
                 ridge = 0.73, ridge_width = 0.5, ridge_rise = 0.2,
                 inner = (:inner23, :inner30), footprint = (:outer50,),
                 ridge_components = (:wall,), wall = nothing, footprint_z = nothing,
                 edge = 0.05, nr = 12, nangle = 24, ncone = 13)

Inner-beam absorption in the wall bubble (a LaserPlasmaModel). The outer cones' picket launches a bubble of wall plasma from the wall under their footprint. Its tip has moved

x_bal(t) = v_b · max(0, t − t_L),   t_L = turn_on + delay,
v_b = speed · energy^0.2 · (fill / 0.3)^-0.2

in from the initial wall radius R (mm, ns; energy is the outer picket fluence over the reference design's, fill the gas fill in mg/cc) while it is ballistic. It stagnates smoothly at the depth stagnation xmax ([`bubbledepth](@ref)): ballistic up to x_max − w (w =stagnationwidth`), then xmax − w·exp(−(xbal − xmax + w)/w). In the plane z_b of the outer footprint, at the axial position where the footprint cones' beam axes reach the wall, the bubble fills the annulus from R in to radius R − x_bub. Each inner beam loses

absorbed = f · (1 − T_b),
T_b = exp(−τ_b (fill/0.3)^q (x_bub/x_ref)^p (R/(R − x_bub))²),

where f is the fraction of its power crossing z_b inside the bubble, computed from the beam itself (its best-focus spot carried along its ncone f-cone directions, as deposition does), and T_b the bubble's transmission (opacity τb, `densityexponentq,exponentp). The absorbed power is conducted to the wall under the outer footprint (Ralph et al. 2018): it is shared equally by thefootprint` beams of the inner beam's hemisphere (those whose port is on the same side), so it is deposited where those beams land. Beam powers are conserved: the model moves power between beams and loses none. (Whatever fraction of the footprint beams themselves misses the wall, a few 1e-4 through the entrance holes, applies to the moved power too.)

Ridge stop. After the ablator–wall collision at collision tc, the inner power the bubble leaves stops at the stagnation ridge, radius ridge·R about the axis (Izumi 2023), instead of reaching the wall. A fraction g(t) of it ([`ridgefraction](@ref), rising over ±ridgerise` ns about tc) is deposited, by lpi_deposit, on an axisymmetric band of the ridge_components (the hohlraum wall) centred on the height where the beam's axis crosses that radius, Gaussian in z with rms ridge_width (mm), area-weighted: the heated ridge radiates at the wall's conversion, from the wall just outside it. The rest of the beam's power is deposited as usual. Power is conserved (the ridge power is booked to the beam's per_beam). The ridge stop acts only where the deposition goes through lpi_deposit: drive_history, Scenario evaluations, the beam-error sensitivities (mode_sensitivity and the rest of uncertainty.jl) and influence_matrix.

The edge of the bubble is smoothed over edge (mm, a logistic step), which keeps f smooth in time, in the bubble depth and in the beams' pointing. Spot points: nr equal-power radii by nangle angles per f-cone direction. Everything is generic in the number type: speed, opacity, stagnation and collision (and the beams) can carry ForwardDiff duals (remake), so the model enters drive_history(...; lpi), Scenario(...; lpi) and calibrations. Compose with CBET as lpi = (cbet, bubble) (transfer first, then the bubble takes its share of what the inner beams carry). wall, a WallMotion, moves the footprint plane with the radiating wall (the outer beams' axes reach the moved wall nearer the entrance hole); the bubble tip and the ridge are measured from the initial wall either way. footprint_z fixes |z_b| instead. stagnation = Inf keeps the tip ballistic; collision = Inf turns the ridge stop off.

Defaults (research notes, "Hohlraum dynamics calibrated to published data (2026-10-24)"; the data are izumi2021_bubble and izumi2021_absorption):

  • speed 0.36 mm/ns at 0.3 mg/cc and the basecamp picket, ∝ E^0.2 ρ₀^-0.2, and delay 0.25 ns after the outer turn-on: fitted to the 28 measured tip positions of Izumi et al., PoP 28, 022706 (2021) Fig. 8a (seven HDC SymCap shots, 0.15–0.3 mg/cc, picket 0.73–1.5×; χ²/dof 0.89 with the paper's ±40 µm): 0.360 (0.347–0.372) mm/ns, delay 0.25 (0.07–0.42) ns, the two correlated. Meezan et al. 2020 measured 321–461 µm/ns on the same shots.
  • density_exponent q = 1.8: inverse-bremsstrahlung absorption goes as ρbub², and Izumi's Eq. 7 has ρbub = S ρ₀ R/(R − x) with the shock compression S ∝ ρ₀^-0.1, so ρbub² ∝ ρ₀^1.8 (R/(R − x))². exponent 0.5 is Eq. 6's chord (xbub/1 mm)^0.5.
  • opacity τb 0.111 is fitted to the inner-power fraction absorbed in the bubble in Izumi's 2D HYDRA simulation of the 0.3 mg/cc basecamp (Fig. 10a: 0.02, 0.23, 0.55 at 4, 5, 6 ns), with this model's own clipping of NIF beams pointed for a 5.75 mm hohlraum and the bubble plane at Izumi's z = ±3 mm (`footprintz = 3.0`): absorbed 0.03, 0.20, 0.36, 0.56 at 4, 5, 5.5, 6 ns, χ² 1.1 at ±0.03. Statistical range 0.100–0.124; the plane (2.8–3.2 mm) and the launch move τb over 0.085–0.158 but leave the absorbed fraction within 0.01, which is the calibrated quantity. Tb at 5.5 ns is then 0.36 at 0.3 mg/cc and 0.58 at 0.15 (Izumi's own model: 0.29 and 0.54, with an annulus for f).
  • stagnation 0.80 R, stagnation_width 0.08 R (engineering): the tip is exactly ballistic to 0.72 R (2.07 mm in a 2.88 mm hohlraum), past the measured depths at 0.3 mg/cc (2.07 mm at 6.2 ns) and near those at 0.15 (up to 2.28 mm, 0.79 R). Izumi 2023: the tip slows on back-pressure near the axis. (R − 0.73 R, the ridge's depth at the waist, would stop the tip before it reaches the inner beams at z_b, 1.3 mm from the wall.)
  • collision tc = turnon + 5.5 ns · (R − rcap)/(2.875 − 0.908 mm) · energy^-0.16 (fitted, Izumi et al., RSI 89, 10K111, 2018: the inner spots separate at 5.8 ns for the basecamp's 0.3 ns turn-on, 5.45 ns with a 1.5× picket, and about 0.4 ns earlier with the 1.18× capsule; the gap between the capsule's outer radius `capsuleradiusr_cap and the wall gives 0.44 ns for that capsule, so a constant closing speed of ablator and wall plasma, 0.36 mm/ns, explains the capsule shot).ridge0.73: Izumi 2023's ridge at r ≈ 2.1 mm in a 2.88 mm hohlraum.ridgewidth0.5 mm andridgerise` 0.2 ns are engineering choices (not measured).

Briareus.CBET — Type
CBET(; leh_z, ne_nc = 0.06, Te = 5000, Ti = 1500, ions = CH_IONS, mach = 1.0,
     flow = nothing, length = 1.5, polarization = :smoothed, multiplier = 1.0,
     nsteps = 20)

Reduced steady-state CBET among the beams that share each laser entrance hole, applied as a laser–plasma model (apply_lpi, Scenario(...; lpi)).

  • leh_z — z (mm) of the LEH planes; beams whose ports have z > 0 cross at +leh_z, the rest at -leh_z.
  • Plasma in the crossing region: ne_nc (electron density over the critical density at the nominal wavelength), Te, Ti (eV), and ion species ions ((fraction, Z, A) number fractions; default fully ionized CH). Defaults are the NIF LEH conditions of Michel et al. 2009; Ti is not given there and is an assumption.
  • flow — outflow speed along the hohlraum axis (km/s, away from the hohlraum), or mach times the ion sound speed √((Z̄ Te + 3 Ti)/(Ā m_p)) when flow is nothing. Signed: a negative mach or flow flows into the hohlraum. With outflow the inner (steeper) cones see the larger blue shift, so flow alone moves power from the inner to the outer cones; red-detuned inner cones (dlambda > 0) take power back. TwoZoneCBET puts a supersonic zone outside the LEH and a slow one inside.
  • length — maximum interaction length (mm). A pair crossing at angle θ interacts over min(length, w/sin θ), with w the mean beam width at the LEH plane.
  • polarization — :smoothed (NIF polarization smoothing, uncorrelated speckle: gain × (1+cos²θ)/4) or :parallel (co-polarized, gain × 1).
  • multiplier — calibration factor on all gains. Fit it to shot data: published NIF designs tune CBET with saturation clamps and power multipliers.
  • clamp — ion-wave saturation level δn/n (nothing = linear theory, which grossly overpredicts transfer at NIF intensities). Each pair's coupling is multiplied by 1/√(1 + (δn/clamp)²), a smooth version of capping the linear δn/n at clamp. Published NIF calibrations range from 3×10⁻⁴ (2011) to 0.002 (foot) / 0.0075 (peak) for N221204 (Kritcher et al., PRE 109, 025204, 2024); default 0.002.

Any plasma parameter and multiplier may be a number, a function of time (ns), or a (times, values) table. Each pair's exchange uses the geometric-mean beam area at the LEH, so total power is conserved. The coupled exchange is integrated with nsteps RK4 steps. Beam detuning comes from Beam.dlambda (Å at the beam's own wavelength — NIF quotes Δλ at 1ω, so divide those values by 3 for 3ω beams).

The pair coefficients, and the two LEHs' exchanges, run on threads; results are bitwise independent of the thread count.

source
Briareus.TwoZoneCBET — Type
TwoZoneCBET(outside::CBET, inside::CBET)
TwoZoneCBET(; leh_z, offset = 0.5, outside = (;), inside = (;))

CBET in two crossing zones per entrance hole, applied in the order the beams meet them: outside the LEH (supersonic outflow; the flow's Doppler shift moves power from the inner cones to the outer ones even without detuning) and inside it (slow flow; the transfer is set by the wavelength detuning, outer → inner for red-detuned inner cones). Each zone is a full CBET with its own crossing plane, flow (signed mach or flow), plasma state (ne_nc, Te, Ti, ions, as numbers, functions of time or (times, values) tables), clamp, multiplier and length, and stays generic in the number type.

The keyword form places the zones offset (mm) outside and inside the LEH planes ±leh_z and passes outside/inside (NamedTuples) to CBET; their defaults are Mach 2 outflow outside (the near-sonic LEH flow turns supersonic outside, Michel et al.

  1. and Mach 0 inside — engineering defaults, not measurements. In a low-fill

hohlraum the net transfer is the balance of the two (Pickworth et al. 2020's HYDRA: inner → outer outside the LEH, outer → inner inside), which is why Δλ = 0 is not zero transfer there (NC24: I-Raum needed ~0.5 Å to cancel flow-driven CBET). n221204_cbet is a version calibrated to the N221204 HYDRA transfer.

Briareus.calibrate_cbet — Method
calibrate_cbet(model, beams, data; receivers, fit = (:multiplier,), time = 0.0,
               bounds = Dict(:multiplier => (1e-3, 10.0), :mach => (0.0, 3.0),
                             :clamp => (1e-4, 0.05))) -> (model, result)

Fit CBET calibration parameters to measured or reference transfer data. data is a list of (dlambda, ratio) pairs: with the receivers groups (e.g. the inner cones) detuned by dlambda (Å, beam wavelength — 1ω-quoted NIF values ÷ 3), their total power should be multiplied by ratio (e.g. 1.6 for "inner +60%"). Fits the constant-valued fields in fit (any of :multiplier, :mach, :clamp) by bounded L-BFGS-B on squared log-ratio residuals with automatic-differentiation gradients. Returns the calibrated model and the Optim result.

Briareus.calibrate_cbet — Method
calibrate_cbet(model, beams_at, data; receivers, fit = (:multiplier,), bounds,
               iterations = 200) -> (model, result)

Multi-epoch calibration: fit model (a CBET or TwoZoneCBET) to a time series of post-transfer power fractions. beams_at(t) gives the setup beams at time t (ns); data lists (t, fraction) or (t, fraction, weight) rows, fraction being the receivers groups' share of the total power after model (e.g. the inner cone fraction of PRE 109 Fig. 9b). Minimizes the weighted sum of squared fraction residuals.

fit names the parameters: fields of a CBET (:multiplier, :mach, :flow, :clamp, :Te, :Ti, :ne_nc), or for a TwoZoneCBET (zone, field) pairs with zone :outside, :inside or :both (one value shared by the two zones; a bare field name means :both). A field holding a number is one parameter; a field holding a (times, values) table fits every value at its fixed time, so plasma-state histories are calibration inputs. bounds maps fields to (lo, hi); parameters with lo > 0 are fitted in log space. Bounded L-BFGS-B with forward-mode gradients.

cbet_coupling(Ω, k, kdotu; ne, Te, Ti, ions) -> Γ
