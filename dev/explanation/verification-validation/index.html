Verification and validation

The tutorials and the test suite carry out four different activities, and their results mean different things.

  • Code verification asks whether the implementation solves the intended discrete equations. Examples are exact view factors of catalog geometries, power conservation of the ray trace, symmetry selection rules that must hold to round-off, and derivatives checked against finite differences.
  • Solution verification asks how large the discretization error of a given calculation is. Examples are the closure error of a view-factor matrix and its convergence with mesh refinement, and the agreement of the fast deposition operator with the ray trace.
  • Validation asks whether the model reproduces an independent reference, either physical or analytic, for the intended use. Examples are the integrating-sphere multiplier, the exact convolution of a laser spot with its f-cone, and, eventually, comparisons with VisRad calculations and shot data.
  • Calibration fits model parameters to reference data. The cross-beam energy transfer model is calibrated, not validated: its multiplier, flow and saturation clamp are fitted to measured transfer.

A tutorial states which of these it does in its closing section, "What this checks".
