Laser–plasma models

Corrections applied between the beam setup and the deposition, differentiable in power and Δλ: per-cone, time-dependent Backscatter; an empirical CalibratedTransfer (transfer as a function of Δλ); a reduced kinetic CBET model at the LEHs (multi-species Maxwellian ion-acoustic response, flow Doppler shift, polarization smoothing and a smooth δn/n saturation clamp), whose multiplier, flow and clamp calibrate_cbet fits to transfer data; TwoZoneCBET, which separates the flowing plasma outside the LEH from the stagnant one inside, with calibrations to published HYDRA transfer for HYBRID-E shots; and inner-beam absorption in the gold bubble (BubbleAbsorption). lpi_deposit applies them together. The CBET model assumes uniform LEH plasma and full pairwise overlap, and its unsaturated coupling overpredicts transfer: its magnitudes hold only after calibration against shot data or reference transfer points.

Backscatter(:inner23 => 0.10, :inner30 => ([0, 5, 8], [0.0, 0.05, 0.12]))

Briareus.CalibratedTransfer — Type
CalibratedTransfer(donors, receivers, fraction)

Empirical cross-beam transfer: a fraction of the total power of the donors groups moves to the receivers groups (shared in proportion to each receiving beam's power). fraction(Δλ, t) is a calibration — e.g. fit to shot data — where Δλ = ⟨dlambda⟩_receivers − ⟨dlambda⟩_donors (Å, power-weighted means), so a positive Δλ means the receivers are red-shifted relative to the donors, the direction that drives transfer toward them. Total power is conserved.

Build fraction with linear_transfer or tabulated_transfer, or pass any differentiable function.

Briareus.kemp2026_sbs — Method
kemp2026_sbs() -> NamedTuple

Measured inner-cone SBS backscatter of N210808 per quad against time: Kemp et al., PoP 33, 082705 (2026) Fig. 2, digitized (data/kemp2026_fig2.csv, provenance in its header). Columns t (ns, 4.4–7.1 every 0.05), inner23 and inner30: all backscattered SBS power of one 23° or 30° quad (TW; the in-lens signal × 3.4), cone-averaged over both hemispheres. The paper's energies are 2.8 and 0.9 kJ per quad (eight quads per cone); single-shot errors reach 100 %, N210808 repeats vary by 30–50 %. SBS starts as the inner power reaches its peak (the pre-CBET quad power is at half its 9 TW peak at 4.74 ns on this axis), peaks at 5.3 (23°) and 5.75 ns (30°) and ends by 6.6–6.9 ns, the 30° cone first (refraction and absorption by an on-axis density spike, the paper's §III C). Used by examples/n221204.jl's backscatter=history. Research notes, "Inner-cone SBS time histories and the late-time refraction (2026-10-25)".

Briareus.BubbleAbsorption — Type
BubbleAbsorption(; radius, turn_on = 0.0, fill = 0.3, picket_ratio = 1.0, kwargs...)

Inner-beam absorption in the wall bubble (a LaserPlasmaModel). The outer cones' picket launches a bubble of wall plasma from the wall under their footprint. Its tip has moved

x_bal(t) = v_b · max(0, t − t_L),   t_L = turn_on + delay,
v_b = speed · E^0.2 · (fill / 0.3)^-0.2

in from the initial wall radius R (mm, ns; E = picket_ratio, the outer picket fluence over the reference design's, dimensionless; fill the gas fill in mg/cc) while it is ballistic. It stagnates smoothly at the depth stagnation $x_{max}$ (bubble_depth): ballistic up to $x_{max} - w$ (w = stagnation_width), then $x_{max} - w e^{-(x_{bal} - x_{max} + w)/w}$. In the plane z_b of the outer footprint, at the axial position where the footprint cones' beam axes reach the wall, the bubble fills the annulus from R in to radius R − $x_{bub}$. Each inner beam loses

absorbed = f · (1 − T_b),
T_b = exp(−τ_b (fill/0.3)^q (x_bub/x_ref)^p (R/(R − x_bub))²),

where f is the fraction of its power crossing z_b inside the bubble, computed from the beam itself (its best-focus spot carried along its ncone f-cone directions, as deposition does), and T_b the bubble's transmission (opacity $τ_b$; the exponent q = density_exponent of the fill density, p = depth_exponent of the depth). The absorbed power is conducted to the wall under the outer footprint (Ralph et al. 2018): it is shared equally by the footprint beams of the inner beam's hemisphere (those whose port is on the same side), so it is deposited where those beams land. Beam powers are conserved: the model moves power between beams and loses none. (Whatever fraction of the footprint beams themselves misses the wall, a few 1e-4 through the entrance holes, applies to the moved power too.)

Ridge stop. After the ablator–wall collision at collision $t_c$, the inner power the bubble leaves stops at the stagnation ridge, radius ridge·R about the axis (Izumi 2023), instead of reaching the wall. A fraction g(t) of it (ridge_fraction, rising over ±ridge_rise ns about $t_c$) is deposited, by lpi_deposit, on an axisymmetric band of the ridge_components (the hohlraum wall) centered on the height where the beam's axis crosses that radius, Gaussian in z with rms ridge_width (mm), area-weighted: the heated ridge radiates at the wall's conversion, from the wall just outside it. The rest of the beam's power is deposited as usual. Power is conserved (the ridge power is booked to the beam's per_beam). The ridge stop acts only where the deposition goes through lpi_deposit: drive_history, Scenario evaluations, the beam-error sensitivities (mode_sensitivity and the rest of uncertainty.jl) and influence_matrix.

The edge of the bubble is smoothed over edge (mm, a logistic step), which keeps f smooth in time, in the bubble depth and in the beams' pointing. Everything is generic in the number type: speed, opacity, stagnation and collision (and the beams) can carry ForwardDiff duals (remake), so the model enters drive_history(...; lpi), Scenario(...; lpi) and calibrations. Compose with CBET as lpi = (cbet, bubble) (transfer first, then the bubble takes its share of what the inner beams carry).

Keywords, by role (defaults in parentheses):

  • Shot and geometry: radius R (required; the initial wall radius, mm), turn_on (0.0; the outer cones' turn-on, ns), fill (0.3 mg/cc), picket_ratio E (1.0), capsule_radius $r_{cap}$ (0.908 mm), inner ((:inner23, :inner30)) and footprint ((:outer50,)), the beam groups that lose and receive the power; wall (nothing), a WallMotion that moves the footprint plane with the radiating wall (the outer beams' axes reach the moved wall nearer the entrance hole; the bubble tip and the ridge are measured from the initial wall either way); footprint_z (nothing), which fixes |$z_b$| instead.
  • Calibration: speed (0.36 mm/ns), delay (0.25 ns), opacity $τ_b$ (0.111), density_exponent q (1.8), depth_exponent p (0.5) and its reference depth x_ref (1.0 mm).
  • Tip stagnation: stagnation (0.80 R) and stagnation_width (0.08 R), both defaulting to fractions of radius; stagnation = Inf keeps the tip ballistic.
  • Ridge sub-model: collision $t_c$ (derived from the other keywords, below; collision = Inf turns the ridge stop off), ridge (0.73), ridge_width (0.5 mm), ridge_rise (0.2 ns), ridge_components ((:wall,)).
  • Numerics: edge (0.05 mm); spot points, nr (12) equal-power radii by nangle (24) angles per f-cone direction, over ncone (13) directions.

Defaults (research notes, "Hohlraum dynamics calibrated to published data (2026-10-24)"; the data are izumi2021_bubble and izumi2021_absorption):

  • speed 0.36 mm/ns at 0.3 mg/cc and the basecamp picket, ∝ E^0.2 ρ₀^-0.2, and delay 0.25 ns after the outer turn-on: fitted to the 28 measured tip positions of Izumi et al., PoP 28, 022706 (2021) Fig. 8a (seven HDC SymCap shots, 0.15–0.3 mg/cc, picket 0.73–1.5×; χ²/dof 0.89 with the paper's ±40 µm): 0.360 (0.347–0.372) mm/ns, delay 0.25 (0.07–0.42) ns, the two correlated. Meezan et al. 2020 measured 321–461 µm/ns on the same shots.
  • density_exponent q = 1.8: inverse-bremsstrahlung absorption goes as $ρ_{bub}^2$, and Izumi's Eq. 7 has $ρ_{bub}$ = S ρ₀ R/(R − x) with the shock compression S ∝ ρ₀^-0.1, so $ρ_{bub}^2$ ∝ ρ₀^1.8 (R/(R − x))². depth_exponent 0.5 is Eq. 6's chord ($x_{bub}$/1 mm)^0.5.
  • opacity $τ_b$ 0.111 is fitted to the inner-power fraction absorbed in the bubble in Izumi's 2D HYDRA simulation of the 0.3 mg/cc basecamp (Fig. 10a: 0.02, 0.23, 0.55 at 4, 5, 6 ns), with this model's own clipping of NIF beams pointed for a 5.75 mm hohlraum and the bubble plane at Izumi's z = ±3 mm (footprint_z = 3.0): absorbed 0.03, 0.20, 0.36, 0.56 at 4, 5, 5.5, 6 ns, χ² 1.1 at ±0.03. Statistical range 0.100–0.124; the plane (2.8–3.2 mm) and the launch move $τ_b$ over 0.085–0.158 but leave the absorbed fraction within 0.01, which is the calibrated quantity. $T_b$ at 5.5 ns is then 0.36 at 0.3 mg/cc and 0.58 at 0.15 (Izumi's own model: 0.29 and 0.54, with an annulus for f).
  • stagnation 0.80 R, stagnation_width 0.08 R (engineering): the tip is exactly ballistic to 0.72 R (2.07 mm in a 2.88 mm hohlraum), past the measured depths at 0.3 mg/cc (2.07 mm at 6.2 ns) and near those at 0.15 (up to 2.28 mm, 0.79 R). Izumi 2023: the tip slows on back-pressure near the axis. (R − 0.73 R, the ridge's depth at the waist, would stop the tip before it reaches the inner beams at $z_b$, 1.3 mm from the wall.)
  • collision $t_c$ = turn_on + 5.5 ns · (R − $r_{cap}$)/(2.875 − 0.908 mm) · E^-0.16 (fitted, Izumi et al., RSI 89, 10K111, 2018: the inner spots separate at 5.8 ns for the basecamp's 0.3 ns turn-on, 5.45 ns with a 1.5× picket, and about 0.4 ns earlier with the 1.18× capsule; the gap between the capsule's outer radius capsule_radius $r_{cap}$ and the wall gives 0.44 ns for that capsule, so a constant closing speed of ablator and wall plasma, 0.36 mm/ns, accounts for the capsule shot). ridge 0.73: Izumi 2023's ridge at r ≈ 2.1 mm in a 2.88 mm hohlraum. ridge_width 0.5 mm and ridge_rise 0.2 ns are engineering choices (not measured).

Briareus.CBET — Type
CBET(; leh_z, ne_nc = 0.06, Te = 5000, Ti = 1500, ions = CH_IONS, mach = 1.0,
     flow = nothing, length = 1.5, polarization = :smoothed, multiplier = 1.0,
     nsteps = 20)

Reduced steady-state CBET among the beams that share each laser entrance hole, applied as a laser–plasma model (apply_lpi, Scenario(...; lpi)).

  • leh_z — z (mm) of the LEH planes; beams whose ports have z > 0 cross at +leh_z, the rest at -leh_z.
  • Plasma in the crossing region: ne_nc (electron density over the critical density at the nominal wavelength), Te, Ti (eV), and ion species ions ((fraction, Z, A) number fractions; default fully ionized CH). Defaults are the NIF LEH conditions of Michel et al. 2009; Ti is not given there and is an assumption.
  • flow — outflow speed along the hohlraum axis (km/s, away from the hohlraum), or mach times the ion sound speed √((Z̄ Te + 3 Ti)/(Ā m_p)) when flow is nothing. Signed: a negative mach or flow flows into the hohlraum. With outflow the inner (steeper) cones see the larger blue shift, so flow alone moves power from the inner to the outer cones; red-detuned inner cones (dlambda > 0) take power back. TwoZoneCBET puts a supersonic zone outside the LEH and a slow one inside.
  • length — maximum interaction length (mm). A pair crossing at angle θ interacts over min(length, w/sin θ), with w the mean beam width at the LEH plane.
  • polarization — :smoothed (NIF polarization smoothing, uncorrelated speckle: gain × (1+cos²θ)/4) or :parallel (co-polarized, gain × 1).
  • multiplier — calibration factor on all gains. Fit it to shot data: published NIF designs tune CBET with saturation clamps and power multipliers.
  • clamp — ion-wave saturation level δn/n (nothing = linear theory, which grossly overpredicts transfer at NIF intensities). Each pair's coupling is multiplied by 1/√(1 + (δn/clamp)²), a smooth version of capping the linear δn/n at clamp. Published NIF calibrations range from 3×10⁻⁴ (2011) to 0.002 (foot) / 0.0075 (peak) for N221204 (Kritcher et al., PRE 109, 025204, 2024); default 0.002. cbet_clamp_schedule builds the foot/peak schedule from a PulseShape.
  • saturation — how clamp acts: :smooth (default, the factor above) or :min, the hard limit δn = min(clamp, δn_linear) of Strozzi et al., PRL 118, 025002 (2017), i.e. a factor min(1, clamp/δn), as in HYDRA's inline model, so its clamp values carry over one to one. At δn = clamp the smooth factor is √2 lower.

Any plasma parameter and multiplier may be a number, a function of time (ns), or a (times, values) table. Each pair's exchange uses the geometric-mean beam area at the LEH, so total power is conserved. The coupled exchange is integrated with nsteps RK4 steps. Beam detuning comes from Beam.dlambda (Å at the beam's own wavelength — NIF quotes Δλ at 1ω, so divide those values by 3 for 3ω beams).

The pair coefficients, and the two LEHs' exchanges, run on threads; results are bitwise independent of the thread count.

Briareus.TwoZoneCBET — Type
TwoZoneCBET(outside::CBET, inside::CBET)
TwoZoneCBET(; leh_z, offset = 0.5, outside = (;), inside = (;))

CBET in two crossing zones per entrance hole, applied in the order in which the beams meet them: outside the LEH (supersonic outflow; the flow's Doppler shift moves power from the inner cones to the outer ones even without detuning) and inside it (slow flow; the transfer is set by the wavelength detuning, outer → inner for red-detuned inner cones). Each zone is a full CBET with its own crossing plane, flow (signed mach or flow), plasma state (ne_nc, Te, Ti, ions, as numbers, functions of time or (times, values) tables), clamp, multiplier and length, and stays generic in the number type.

The keyword form places the zones offset (mm) outside and inside the LEH planes ±leh_z and passes outside/inside (NamedTuples) to CBET; their defaults are Mach 2 outflow outside (the near-sonic LEH flow turns supersonic outside, Michel et al.

  1. and Mach 0 inside — engineering defaults, not measurements. In a low-fill

hohlraum the net transfer is the balance of the two (Pickworth et al. 2020's HYDRA: inner → outer outside the LEH, outer → inner inside), which is why Δλ = 0 is not zero transfer there (NC24: I-Raum needed ~0.5 Å to cancel flow-driven CBET). n221204_cbet is a version calibrated to the N221204 HYDRA transfer.

Briareus.calibrate_cbet — Method
calibrate_cbet(model, beams, data; receivers, fit = (:multiplier,), time = 0.0,
               bounds = Dict(:multiplier => (1e-3, 10.0), :mach => (0.0, 3.0),
                             :clamp => (1e-4, 0.05))) -> (model, result)

Fit CBET calibration parameters to measured or reference transfer data. data is a list of (dlambda, ratio) pairs: with the receivers groups (e.g. the inner cones) detuned by dlambda (Å, beam wavelength — 1ω-quoted NIF values ÷ 3), their total power should be multiplied by ratio (e.g. 1.6 for "inner +60%"). Fits the constant-valued fields in fit (any of :multiplier, :mach, :clamp) by bounded L-BFGS-B on squared log-ratio residuals with automatic-differentiation gradients. Returns the calibrated model and the Optim result.

Briareus.calibrate_cbet — Method
calibrate_cbet(model, shots::AbstractVector; receivers, fit = (:multiplier,), bounds,
               penalty = nothing, iterations = 200) -> (model, result)

Joint multi-shot calibration: one model (shared plasma physics) fitted to the post-transfer power fractions of several shots at once. Each entry of shots is a NamedTuple (; beams_at, data, delay = 0.0): beams_at(t) gives that shot's setup beams (its own powers and detuning) and data its (t, fraction[, weight]) rows, as in the multi-epoch method. delay (ns) shifts the shot against the model's time tables: the model is evaluated at t - delay, so a shot whose rise to peak comes 0.2 ns earlier than the reference pulse's has delay = -0.2 and sees the reference's plasma history (and clamp schedule) 0.2 ns early. penalty(model) (optional) is added to the loss, e.g. a constraint on the Δλ null; it must be generic in the number type. fit, bounds: as in the multi-epoch method.

source
Briareus.calibrate_cbet — Method
calibrate_cbet(model, beams_at, data; receivers, fit = (:multiplier,), bounds,
               iterations = 200) -> (model, result)

Multi-epoch calibration: fit model (a CBET or TwoZoneCBET) to a time series of post-transfer power fractions. beams_at(t) gives the setup beams at time t (ns); data lists (t, fraction) or (t, fraction, weight) rows, fraction being the receivers groups' share of the total power after model (e.g. the inner cone fraction of PRE 109 Fig. 9b). Minimizes the weighted sum of squared fraction residuals.

fit names the parameters: fields of a CBET (:multiplier, :mach, :flow, :clamp, :Te, :Ti, :ne_nc), or for a TwoZoneCBET (zone, field) pairs with zone :outside, :inside or :both (one value shared by the two zones; a bare field name means :both). A field holding a number is one parameter; a field holding a (times, values) table fits every value at its fixed time, so plasma-state histories are calibration inputs. bounds maps fields to (lo, hi); parameters with lo > 0 are fitted in log space. Bounded L-BFGS-B with forward-mode gradients.

Briareus.cbet_clamp_schedule — Method
cbet_clamp_schedule(pulse; foot = 0.002, peak = 0.0075, width = 0.2) -> (times, values)

CBET saturation clamp δn/n stepping from foot to peak over width (ns) centered on the start of the pulse's rise to peak power (pulse_rise), as a two-knot (times, values) table for CBET's clamp (constant beyond the knots, so fitting :clamp fits the foot and peak values). Briareus.hybride_cbet — Method
hybride_cbet(; leh_z = 5.62, offset = 0.5, delay = 0.0) -> TwoZoneCBET

Two-zone CBET fitted jointly to HYDRA's post-transfer cone fraction of three HYBRID-E shots at three detunings: N210808 (1.8 Å, PRE 109 Fig. 1e with PRE 106 Fig. 1d's incident powers, pre109_fig1, n210808_fig1), N220919 (2.5 Å, n220919_fig9) and N221204 (2.75 Å, n221204_fig9), with one plasma history for all, under two published constraints: Te ≤ 5.5 keV (the LEH values of Michel 2009 and Barrios 2016) and the Δλ null inside NC24's 1–1.55 Å for HYBRID-E (that band is the Δλ of round hot spots in the 2020–21 shots, not the transfer null: hybride_cbet_iraum holds the null at I-Raum's ≤ 0.5 Å instead). HYDRA's hard-minimum saturation (saturation = :min) with the clamp schedule switched at the rise (cbet_clamp_schedule) and both clamp values fitted: 1.4×10⁻³ in the foot (not binding, unconstrained), 4.8×10⁻⁴ at peak (binding; ±27 %). Outside: Mach 1.54, multiplier 0.14 (×/÷ 2); inside: Mach 0, multiplier 0.73 (±18 %); Te(t) 2.1 keV at 1 ns, 2.8 at 4, 3.4–3.9 in the peak, 5.5 (the bound) at 8 ns (±6–14 %).

Fit (107 rows, 0.8–8.4 ns): cone-fraction rms 0.027 (N221204), 0.016 (N220919), 0.047 (N210808), against 0.016 / 0.030 / 0.068 for n221204_cbet; null 0.97 ± 0.10 Å at 5.6 ns. It does not reproduce HYDRA's flat cross-shot response: dCF/dΔλ at peak is 0.17–0.20 per Å (HYDRA's N220919 and N221204 differ by 0.03–0.06 per Å), so N210808's peak is 0.07–0.10 low and N221204's 0.03 high. delay (ns) shifts the time tables for a pulse whose rise comes delay later than N221204's (negative: earlier).

Derived (research notes, "Joint multi-shot CBET calibration (2026-10-24)"): effective parameters fitted to HYDRA, not a measured plasma state.

Briareus.hybride_cbet_iraum — Method
hybride_cbet_iraum(; leh_z = 5.62, offset = 0.5, delay = 0.0) -> TwoZoneCBET

The joint three-shot fit of hybride_cbet (same data, model, Te ≤ 5.5 keV bound and fitted hard-minimum clamps) with the Δλ null constraint corrected: NC24's 1–1.55 Å for HYBRID-E is the detuning that minimized hot-spot P2 in the 2020–21 shots, which also compensates the inner beams' bubble absorption, so it lies above the transfer null; the published statement on the null itself is I-Raum's (0.5 Å "mostly to negate the flow-induced CBET", NC24). Here the null at N221204's peak (5.6 and 7.0 ns) is held in 0.25–0.5 Å (the lower edge, keeping a flow-driven transfer at Δλ = 0, is a Briareus choice).

Outside: Mach 1.49 (± 0.8), multiplier 0.072 (×/÷ 5); inside: Mach 0, multiplier 1.60 (± 31 %); clamps 9.1×10⁻⁴ (foot, not binding, unconstrained) and 1.4×10⁻⁴ (peak, binding, ± 29 %; below the 5×10⁻⁴–7.5×10⁻³ of HYDRA decks, with which it trades against the inside multiplier and Te, correlations −0.92 to −0.97); Te(t) 3.2 keV at 1 ns, 4.5 at 4, 2.5–3.8 in the peak, 5.5 (the bound) at 8 ns (± 7–15 %).

Fit (107 rows, 0.8–8.4 ns): cone-fraction rms 0.024 (N221204), 0.016 (N220919), 0.028 (N210808), all 0.024 (hybride_cbet: 0.027 / 0.016 / 0.047, all 0.033); loss 0.059 against 0.120 with the null in 1–1.55 Å. Null 0.24 ± 0.20 Å at 5.6 ns (0.29 at 7 ns); dCF/dΔλ 0.137 ± 0.011 per Å at peak (2.5–2.75 Å), linear from 1 Å. HYDRA's cross-shot response stays 2–3× flatter, which the measured hot-spot P2 sensitivity does not support (research notes). delay: as in hybride_cbet.

Derived (research notes, "Joint multi-shot CBET calibration: the null"): effective parameters fitted to HYDRA, not a measured plasma state.

Briareus.pulse_rise — Method
pulse_rise(pulse; lookback = 1.0, delta = 0.15) -> t

Start of the rise to peak power (ns), pulse a PulseShape or (times, powers): the knee where the tangent at the half-maximum crossing t½ (slope over t½ ± delta) meets the power lookback ns before t½ (the step the rise leaves). It is invariant to a scale factor on the power, so delivered and HYDRA-degraded inputs give nearly the same time: 4.11 ns on N221204's delivered pulse (n221204_pulse), 4.0 on PRE 109 Fig. 9's degraded input powers, where the HYDRA deck switches its CBET clamp ("until the beginning of the rise to peak power", 3.9–4.1 ns). An engineering definition for pulses with one main rise.
